Autonomous Service & Hospitality Robot

A human-scale autonomous service and hospitality robot engineered from the ground up — featuring a custom welded steel chassis, composite outer body shell, interactive digital facial head unit, and payload delivery tray.

Project Overview

In modern hospitality, event venues, and commercial spaces, autonomous service robots bridge the gap between automation and human interaction. They reduce staff fatigue, ensure precise repetitive item transport, and deliver an engaging, welcoming experience for guests.

Our engineering team developed this service robot from scratch, handling everything from structural welding and mechanical fabrication to composite body shaping, motor actuation, electrical harness design, and interactive human-machine interface (HMI).

Key Engineering Modules

Mechanical Chassis & Mobility

Heavy-duty welded tubular steel base plate with caster wheels and high-torque DC geared wheel drives. Rigid central spine provides robust support for composite torso shells and heavy payload trays.

Power & Drive Electronics

High-current motor driver stages with isolated logic power buses, fail-safe emergency cutoffs, and stabilized power supplies for embedded controllers and display screens.

Interactive HMI & Facial Display

Animated digital eyes and expressive facial UI conveying greeting states, driving feedback, and friendly interaction with visitors.

Honors & Recognition

The project was formally recognized on stage at the Kerala Business Conclave 2026 (100 Malayalees Business Stars) at Lulu Twin Tower, Kochi, and commended by the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, Christ College of Engineering (Autonomous).
